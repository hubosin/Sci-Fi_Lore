**Also called:** space whales · **Biochemistry:** silicon-based · **Habitat:** protoplanetary disks and asteroid fields

The Sonorous Leviathan is a giant space-dwelling creature. *Sonorous* means low or deep.

## Life cycle

1. **Microbe.** It starts as a silicon-based microbe living in tiny cracks in rock inside an active protoplanetary disk, feeding on oxidation and small mineral compounds.
2. **Worm.** It grows into a worm-like creature that burrows through porous rock toward hot, mineral-rich pockets.
3. **Armoured form.** Moving toward the outside of asteroids, it grows a hard exoskeleton of rock or mineral to resist vacuum.
4. **Leviathan.** The adult is a kind of **leech** that clings to mineral-rich asteroids and eats the rock.

## Reproduction

Adults have almost no way to move on their own. They release **vacuum-sealed eggs** into space, which drift until they hit rock.
