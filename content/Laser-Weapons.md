Lasers in this universe have **realistic** power output and firing-device size. Small arms still fire bullets, fire and explosives. Lasers are mounted on vehicles, aircraft, ships, satellites and ground installations.

## Classes

| Class | Mounts | Uses | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Small** | Shoulder- or vehicle-mounted, small/light aircraft | Point defence like CIWS against guided missiles and large-calibre (~50 mm+) shells. Very poor offensive capability | ~75–200 kW |
| **Medium** | Small vessels, satellites, dedicated vehicles, large aircraft | Intercepting aircraft, light or unarmoured vehicles, cruise missiles, ballistic missiles in the boost stage; space-to-space warfare | ~1–10 MW |
| **Large** | Vessels, ground installations | Everything above, plus piercing some armour, killing ballistic missiles in the terminal stage, and damaging satellites. At ~100 MW they can destroy satellites and small spacecraft | 25+ MW |

## Defences against lasers

- **Mirrors:** they have to be flawless, so they're mainly useful on spacecraft
- **Reflective coatings:** far more practical, and effective in atmosphere
- **Weather:** fog, rain and cloud all blunt lasers
- **Smoke:** reusable smoke systems on surface vehicles and aircraft

## Fighting against laser defences

- Flying aircraft in bad weather
- Traditional decoys, depending on the guidance system
- Swarm tactics with large numbers of cheap drones
- Fast munitions
- Heat-resistant construction
