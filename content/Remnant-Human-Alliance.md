**Timeline:** Canon · **Formed:** January 6, 1985 · **Also called:** the RH Alliance, the Human-Remnant Alliance

The alliance between [the Remnants](The-Remnants) and the pro-treaty nations of [Humanity](Humanity), formed to prepare for and fight [the Conglomerate](The-Conglomerate).

## Formation

After [First Contact](First-Contact), the [Remnant Monarch](Remnant-Monarch) and elected officials met Earth's leaders and addressed the U.N. on **November 20, 1984**. The alliance became official on **January 6, 1985**. The inaugural signees were most NATO members, the USSR, and Japan, with other nations joining later.

## Members

- **Human bloc:** mainly NATO and the Soviet Bloc, along with other assorted nations
- **The Remnants:** the monarch, elected officials, and roughly 14 million survivors

## Products of the alliance

See also [Second Industrial Age](Second-Industrial-Age).

- [First-generation mechs](First-Generation-Mechs): pure technology, designed jointly and built by humans
- The [RHHM](RHHM), a habitat-making device built as a philanthropy project
- A treaty and limited technology trades with [the Virulon](The-Virulon)

## Opposition

See [Opposition Bloc](Opposition-Bloc). The treaty split humanity almost immediately (January 7–9, 1985). The opposition bloc includes countries that left NATO and countries that oppose the alliance. Within it are people who:

- **Don't believe the Remnants exist,** and think governments invented them to restart the war economy (the cause of the May 20, 1985 protests)
- **Distrust the Remnants,** fearing they're manipulating humanity to get its weapons technology
- **Side with the Conglomerate's goals,** a view that grows late in the war as humanity learns what the Conglomerate is fighting

## During the war

The alliance was far stronger than the Conglomerate expected, strong enough that fighting it became a waste of the Conglomerate's time. That is why the Conglomerate offered a compromise instead of a war of extermination. See [The Conglomerate War](Conglomerate-War).
