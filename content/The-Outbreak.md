**Timeline:** [RHV](Timeline-RHV) · **When:** years after the Conglomerate War

The arrival on Earth of [the Ultimate Threat](The-disease), the virus the Conglomerate had been gathering resources to fight.

## Spread

After the war, bio-organic [later-generation mechs](Later-Generation-Mechs) were kept in service for cleanup and reconstruction. When the virus arrived, it spread through them. Every later-generation mech that came into contact with it was eventually turned: the machine mutates, traps its pilot inside under its control, and in time the pilot **fuses** with the mech. The resulting monsters rampage across what is left of the world.

The military initially sent pilots against infected mechs before anyone understood how easily the virus spread, which infected more of them.

## How infection progresses in a pilot

1. An off feeling around the mech that slowly turns into a pained sickness
2. Nightmares, asleep and awake. The pilot feels safe **only** inside the mech, like a craving
3. The pilot's life spirals until they break. The mech integrates their body into itself, and the two grow into one monster
4. Monsters adapt and join a larger **hivemind**, cooperating to cause as much damage as possible

Infected mechs can reject new pilots, injuring or killing them, and can keep moving with **no pilot at all**.

## Resistance

- Purely mechanical machines, including first-generation mechs, are immune to direct infection.
- Some pilots build up a **resistance**. Pilots with a very high affinity are affected far less, and keeping to mandated time outside the mech slows the infection. A pilot who is exposed, leaves their mech, and later gets pulled back in can use that resistance to fight for control.
- Finding what makes a mech resist the virus, and replicating it, is the key to slowing the spread.

## Society

People fear pilots, even uninfected ones. Veteran pilots and their old [first-generation](First-Generation-Mechs) machines return to the fight. Mercenaries and pirates run stolen or salvaged mechs. A large share of survivors live in [The Ring](The-Ring).
