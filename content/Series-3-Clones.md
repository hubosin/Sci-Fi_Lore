**Program:** [BRIDGE Project](BRIDGE-Project) · **Designations:** S31–S39 · **Surrogates:** [Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov), [Nina Lera](Nina-Lera) · **Timeline:** Canon

The **Series 3** pilots are genetically engineered clones of the best [Series 2](Pilot-Series#series-2) outliers, raised inside BRIDGE facilities. All of them view and refer to each other as **siblings**, because of their shared experiences, their closeness and the traits their gene edits gave them.

## Purpose

Series 3 was created to produce pilots artificially: to control the results, make better pilots, and sidestep the human-rights problems of testing on the public. It overlapped with Series 2 rather than replacing it.

## The cloning process

Every clone used the best Series 2 pilots as a genetic reference and was then **heavily modified**.

### Surrogate vs. lab

| Method | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| **Surrogate mother** | Cheaper and faster | Little control over development |
| **Artificial womb (lab)** | Direct adjustments throughout growth; technically superior | Huge time and resource cost |

[Samantha](Samantha) and [Olivia](Olivia) were made from the same embryo, one in each method, to settle which was better. Both survived, so the test didn't answer the question.

### Defects

Most early clones had severe genetic defects from the engineering, and many died young, most before birth. Any clone that survived past **age five** was counted as a success, since a more developed immune system meant a much better chance of survival.

The edits for higher aptitude left every clone with some form of **melanin deficiency**, in some cases albinism. That is why the clones have white, silver or pale blonde hair, pale skin, and red or blue eyes.

### "Imperfect" and "perfect" clones

The clones before S38 are called **imperfect**: high aptitude came with serious health problems. [Mila](Mila) (S38) was the first **perfect** clone, and [Daniel](Daniel) (S39) proved the process could be repeated with any surrogate.

## Labels and names

Only clones that survived until birth were given a number. Each wears an identification label: S31–S34 on the **right inner wrist**, and S35a onward on the **right shoulder**.

[Adam](Adam), the first successful clone, gave every one of his siblings a name based on their number, so no one would be just a number. Once he joined the cloning team, the names were added to the labels themselves. Only the clones and a very few scientists use them.

### Carter (S32)

Carter showed signs of poor health early but got the second-furthest of any clone up to that point before dying at birth. Adam named him with the others, partly because of how far he got and the effect his death had on Lana.

## End of Series 3

Series 3 ended with the death of Daniel (S39), killed by [Nina Lera](Nina-Lera) during [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault). The program then moved on to aptitude-tested volunteers in [Series 4](Pilot-Series#series-4).
