**Timeline:** [EACO](Timeline-EACO) · **Members:** mostly Remnants, plus the humans who refused the Conglomerate's offer

The Survivors are those who stayed on Earth after humanity accepted the Conglomerate's offer and chose to fight what they saw as the greater evil.

## Membership

When humanity joined the Conglomerate, some Remnants reluctantly followed. **Most stayed on Earth**, and they make up the bulk of the Survivors alongside the holdout humans.

## The world they hold

The Conglomerate drained Earth's oceans and mined almost the whole surface. Nearly every landmark is gone, and most evidence of human civilisation is gone with it. The Survivors are almost the only sign of life left.

## Technology and the virus

The Survivors still use the original, purely mechanical **Remnant-Human technology**, which is immune to direct infection by [the Ultimate Threat](The-disease). Because of it they held out through the virus. Prolonged exposure without direct contact, together with cleansing and removal of bio-organics, left their world almost completely free of it, and those who remain have built up some immunity.

## Enemies

The Conglomerate sends forces, including human soldiers woken from [stasis](Stasis), to crush the Survivors. When the Survivors push back hard enough, the Conglomerate abandons its troops rather than recover them.
