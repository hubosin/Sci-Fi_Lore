**Designation:** S33 · **Species:** Human ([Series 3 clone](Series-3-Clones)) · **Born:** surrogate ([Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov)) · **Died:** shortly before age 5 · **Timeline:** Canon

## Appearance

* Long, often braided snow-white hair, very pale skin and red eyes
* Very small and very slim
* **Label:** S33, on her right wrist

## Health

Rose seemed healthy at birth, but her immune system quickly deteriorated and she was often sick. Because she hid her pain so well, the scientists monitoring her didn't realise how serious her condition was until the day she died, of **immune-system failure** caused by genetic defects, shortly before her fifth birthday.

## Personality

Always smiling despite frequent pain. Incredibly creative, and her greatest virtue was bringing happiness to others.

## Relationships

* **[Adam](Adam):** they had a very close sibling bond. Adam never grew as close to any of the other clones after her death.

[Daniel](Daniel) later played a similar role for [Mila](Mila) that Rose played for Adam.
