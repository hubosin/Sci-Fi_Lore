The Remnant-Human Alliance refuses the Conglomerate's compromise and **wins the war at Earth**.

## The victory

The win is real but limited. The Conglomerate's full army is far larger than the force sent to Earth. It simply judges that continuing to fight the alliance is a waste of time and resources. A large part of Earth's surface is left in ruin.

In some accounts the Conglomerate never gets its strongest machines to Earth at all. It lands only a relatively small ground force, still enough to overrun large regions such as [Alaska and the Russian Far East](Alaska-and-the-Russian-Far-East).

## After the war

- Mechs stay in service to hunt leftover Conglomerate forces and help with reconstruction. First-generation mechs are decommissioned and their surviving pilots honourably discharged. Most first-generation pilots died in the war.
- The last supercharged [RHHM](RHHM) creates [The Ring](The-Ring), where a large share of surviving humans live.
- Years later [the Ultimate Threat](The-disease) reaches Earth: see [The Outbreak](The-Outbreak). Later-generation bio-tech mechs are taken over and fused with their pilots, and monster mechs roam what is left of the world.
- Mercenaries and pirates operate stolen or salvaged mechs.
