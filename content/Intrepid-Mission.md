**Type:** crewed Mars landing · **Operators:** NASA / Roscosmos · **Landing:** February 2, 1982 · **Timeline:** Canon

The joint American-Soviet mission that landed the **first humans on Mars**.

## Background

It followed the first crewed Mars flyby, which launched on November 8, 1975.

## Legacy

Two years later, Mars became the holding point for the Remnant fleet. See [Mars](Mars).
