**Began:** January 12, 1985 · **Timeline:** Canon

The alliance-wide industrial build-up that followed the founding of the [Remnant-Human Alliance](Remnant-Human-Alliance), preparing Earth for the Conglomerate.

## Beginning

Six days after the treaty was signed, ground was broken on massive manufacturing sites in **North America** and the **USSR**, building from Remnant-Human blueprints.

## Effects on society

Society as a whole was pointed at one goal. Daily life, homes and consumer goods stayed much as they were in the 1980s, while **military technology, weapons, spaceflight and power generation** leapt ahead.

## Products

- [First-Generation Mechs](First-Generation-Mechs)
- The [RHHM](RHHM)
- Advanced laser, shield and spaceflight technology

## Opposition

The build-up fuelled suspicion. On **May 20, 1985**, mass protests broke out as citizens accused their governments of inventing the Remnants to restart the war economy. See [Opposition Bloc](Opposition-Bloc).
