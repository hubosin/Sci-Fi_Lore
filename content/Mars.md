**Type:** planet · **Timeline:** Canon

## Human exploration

- **November 8, 1975:** the first crewed Mars flyby launches
- **February 2, 1982:** the [*Intrepid*](Intrepid-Mission) mission lands the first humans on Mars

## Remnant holding pattern

After [First Contact](First-Contact), the Remnant fleet, escorted by U.S. and Soviet warships, entered a **holding pattern over Mars** on **October 29, 1984**. Remnant leaders travelled from there to Earth.
