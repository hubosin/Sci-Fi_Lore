**Period:** 1945 onward · **Rivals:** United States vs. USSR · **Timeline:** Canon

This universe's version of the **Cold War**. It was fought as a race for **scientific and spaceflight achievement** rather than an arms race.

## How it differs from real history

- The space race never really ended after Apollo. Apollo-Soyuz (1975) closed the space race, and the rivalry carried on as the science race.
- The USSR never dissolved.
- The worldwide public stayed fascinated by spaceflight.
- NASA got the space shuttle it wanted.

## Weapons

The science race replaced the nuclear arms race, but weapons were still developed. **Classified** weapons advances made during it proved decisive in the [I-Line](Timeline-I-Line).

## After first contact

The rivals became partners. The U.S. and USSR both joined the [Remnant-Human Alliance](Remnant-Human-Alliance) and fought side by side in the [Conglomerate War](Conglomerate-War).
