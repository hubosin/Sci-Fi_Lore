**Faction:** [The Conglomerate](The-Conglomerate) · **Biochemistry:** silicon-based · **Timeline:** Canon

A silicon-based lifeform that needs **extremely cold temperatures** to survive. Its species name has not been recorded.

## Biology

At base the species is extremely durable and thrives in extreme cold. It cannot survive **warmer temperatures** or **Earth's atmosphere**. Space is a good insulator, so it can survive far longer outside its mech in space than on a planet.

## War mechs

Off its homeworld this species is only ever seen inside a **bio-organic mech** linked directly to its brain activity and will, and it is mostly known by these machines.

- The mech is **filled with liquid methane** (or another cold fluid) and heavily insulated.
- Its organic malleability makes it **extremely impact-absorbent**, and it can take multiple hits.
- One or two tank shells in the right place will usually destroy it.
- **Any breach is fatal.** The escaping cold flash-freezes whatever made the hole, and the interior temperature drops rapidly, killing the pilot and wrecking the internals.
