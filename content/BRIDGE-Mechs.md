**Builder:** [BRIDGE Project](BRIDGE-Project) · **Naming:** LPG# M# / BG# M# · **Timeline:** Canon

The mechs and prototypes built by the Link Program and BRIDGE, leading to humanity's [later-generation mechs](Later-Generation-Mechs).

## Designations

Each machine is named by program, generation and model:

| Code | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **LPG# M#** | Link Program, Generation #, Model # | LPG1 M01 |
| **BG# M#** | BRIDGE, Generation #, Model # | BG3 M00 |
| **G#M#** | Short form with the program dropped | G2M01 |
| **(P)** | Prototype | BG3 M00 (P) |

Generation numbers describe **purpose and technology level**, not release date. Generation 4 entered service before Generation 3.

## Generation 0

Every prosthetic the Link Program built before its first prototype mech is counted as **generation 0** (or "less than 1").

## Generation 1

The first wave. Mechanical recreations of what the team observed in Conglomerate neural-link tech, controlled by reading thought patterns from **outside** the pilot's head.

### LPG1 M00 (P)

The direct prototype of LPG1 M01. A small-scale exosuit that **could not hold a pilot**. Instead an operator outside wore a helmet that detected their thought patterns and replicated them as movement, wired directly to the prototype. It combined the team's earlier technology into one product to test larger-scale control.

* **Result:** a success
* **Drawbacks:** needed a direct connection to a large outside power source, and control from outside

### LPG1 M01

A true **wearable exosuit**. It could be adjusted, but only for a range of body sizes. It was bulky, and most of its strength went into carrying itself, yet its arms alone could still lift over **100 kg**. Its control helmet was built into the suit. Like M00 it needed a direct connection to outside power.

A **modified LPG1 M01** was used by [Nina Lera](Nina-Lera) to control [Daniel](Daniel) during [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault).

### LPG1 lab mech

*Model number not yet assigned.*

The Link Program's first large-scale machine, roughly the size of an **upright shipping container**. It barely fit inside the lab it was built in and could never be moved out.

* **Body:** torso, arms and a cockpit, with no lower half
* **Control:** slow, but capable of extremely precise movement with simple controls
* **Power:** wired directly into the lab, since it was never meant to move
* **Hands:** the most advanced part of the machine, built to survive dangerous experiments and Conglomerate tech
* **Shielding:** across the front of the cockpit for extreme reactions; never needed

It was the last Generation 1 mech, built to begin production of Generation 2, and was used on many later projects.

## Generation 2

### LPG2 M01

The first, last and only Generation 2 mech, and the machine that ended the Link Program's independence.

Earlier mechs had only used Conglomerate tech as **reference** for purely mechanical designs. G2M01 was built mainly **from** Conglomerate tech, which is largely biological, with alliance tech added to control and adjust it. It was designed to push the limits of linking, with complex movement to be unlocked slowly through testing.

Because Conglomerate tech is biological, the link had to change too. G2M01 needed **direct access to the pilot's brain** instead of the outside signals Generation 1 used. [The Engineer](The-Engineer) designed the link and volunteered as pilot, and [the Doctor](The-Doctor) and a second doctor secretly performed the surgery.

On start-up a power fluctuation overrode the limits set to reduce strain on the pilot. The Engineer was overwhelmed with pain and mental overload and involuntarily drove G2M01 into a rampage through the lab. Equipment was destroyed but no one outside the mech died. The Engineer was found dead in the cockpit after an exterior shutdown; later examination found **severe damage to the prefrontal cortex**.

During the rampage G2M01 moved faster than any mech before it and showed extreme destructive power under a single pilot. The governments took over the program shortly afterwards.

G2M01 was later **repaired**, and it was the mech at the centre of [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault).

## Generation 3

Generation 3 was planned first, but its prototype started out so close to G2M01 that it had to be almost completely redesigned, and **Generation 4 was released first**. Later Generation 3 mechs were built mainly for testing.

### BG3 M00 (P)

The first direct-link mech after G2M01, built to test that same link system. Unlike most BRIDGE mechs it carries **no weapons or advanced armour plating**.

* **Pilot:** [Nova](Nova) (S37)
* **Nickname:** "Zero"
* **Notable action:** fought the repaired G2M01 during [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault)
