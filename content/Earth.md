**Type:** planet · **Species:** [Humanity](Humanity) · **Timeline:** all

Humanity's homeworld and the prize of the [Conglomerate War](Conglomerate-War). The Conglomerate wants it because it is **rich in resources** and **full of life**, which its bio-mechanical technology needs.

## Earth by timeline

### Canon

At first contact the Remnants chose Earth because it was the closest habitable planet to where their jump came out. After 1985 massive manufacturing sites spread across **North America** and the **USSR** ([Second Industrial Age](Second-Industrial-Age)). During the war, the second Conglomerate attack destroyed most of the world's forests.

### RHV

Earth wins, but much of the surface is in ruin. Survivors cluster around [The Ring](The-Ring) and smaller [RHHMs](RHHM). Years later [the Outbreak](The-Outbreak) sweeps the planet.

### EACO

The Conglomerate **drains the oceans** and mines almost the entire surface. Nearly every landmark and sign of human life is gone. The Statue of Liberty still stands, damaged, rising out of the dry seabed beside a ruined New York. Only [the Survivors](The-Survivors) remain.

### Red Line

Humanity is wiped out and Earth is stripped of its resources.
