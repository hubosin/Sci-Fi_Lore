**Timeline:** EACO (Earth Accepts Conglomerate Offer) · [Branch map](Timelines)

Humanity **accepts** the Conglomerate's offer. It joins the Conglomerate, helps fight [the Ultimate Threat](The-disease), and hands over Earth's resources.

## Aftermath

- Humans who join the Conglomerate leave Earth. Soldiers are held in **stasis** for later deployment to worlds that resist the Conglomerate.
- The Remnants are heavily against the deal. A minority reluctantly follow the humans who leave, but most stay on Earth with the humans who refuse. Together they become [the Survivors](The-Survivors).
- The Conglomerate strips Earth: the **oceans are drained** and almost the whole surface is mined. Nearly every landmark and sign of human life is erased.
- The virus reaches Earth, but the Survivors' purely mechanical technology is immune to direct infection. With no bio-organics left to spread through, the virus mostly dies out, and the people who remain build up some immunity.
- When a world pushes back hard enough, the Conglomerate **abandons its troops** there rather than risk resources, infiltration, or infection by retrieving them.
