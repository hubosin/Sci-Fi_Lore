Faster-than-light travel works differently for each power. Only **ships** travel faster than light; communications don't, so **scout ships** are vital in war.

| Power | Method |
|---|---|
| [Humanity](Humanity) | [Alcubierre Drive](Alcubierre-Drive) |
| [The Remnants](The-Remnants) | [Stargates](Stargates), plus jumps through existing wormholes and weak spots in space-time |
| [The Virulon](The-Virulon) | [Stargates](Stargates) |
| [The Conglomerate](The-Conglomerate) | Copied, volatile [stargates](Stargates) |
