**Type:** virus · **Timeline:** Canon (reaches Earth in [RHV](Timeline-RHV) and [EACO](Timeline-EACO))

The greater threat that [the Conglomerate](The-Conglomerate) exists to fight, and the reason it strips worlds for resources. It takes the form of a **virus**.

## Nature

- It infects **bio-organic** life and technology. Bio-mechanical machines are its main vector.
- It takes control of infected hosts, mutates them, and fuses pilots with their machines into monsters.
- Infected hosts link into a **hivemind** that gains intelligence and strategy over time, especially from hosts that were intelligent pilots.
- It can drive a machine with **no pilot inside**, so a "dead" host may not stay dead.
- Trace amounts can survive in storage, and a stored sample can restart an outbreak.

## Weaknesses

- **Purely mechanical technology is immune** to direct infection. That makes the original Remnant-Human technology a strong counter, though the Conglomerate doesn't see it that way.
- Some individuals develop a **resistance** after exposure, and with enough willpower an infected pilot can take back control. See [The Outbreak](The-Outbreak#resistance).
- Without bio-organics to spread through, and with cleansing and removal, it dies out, and survivors build up some immunity.
