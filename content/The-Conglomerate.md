**Type:** multi-species alliance · **Timeline:** Canon

The Conglomerate is a joint force of alien species united to stop [the Ultimate Threat](The-disease). Its goal is good, but its methods can do more damage than the threat itself.

## Methods

The Conglomerate moves from planet to planet gathering resources. It **prioritises planets with life**, because its technology is bio-mechanical and needs organic material.

It began by allying with the species whose resources it took, absorbing them as members. As time ran short, and as it met species that fought back or weren't advanced enough to join, it started to **kill and take** instead. A species it judges unfit is exterminated and its world strip-mined. That is what happened to [the Remnants](The-Remnants).

When a world pushes back hard, the Conglomerate usually **abandons** the troops it has on the ground rather than spend resources retrieving them or risk infiltration or infection.

## Technology

- **Bio-mechanical and organic technology**, including bio-organic mechs linked directly to their pilots' brains. Humanity's [later-generation mechs](Later-Generation-Mechs) use bio-tech stolen from the Conglomerate.
- **Power:** Dyson spheres and captured technology
- **FTL:** cheaper, more volatile copies of outdated [stargate](Stargates) technology taken from the [Remnant homeworld](Remnant-Homeworld)
- **Motherships** in orbit deploy ground forces
- [Stasis](Stasis) for holding soldiers between deployments, and wormhole transport ships to carry them

## Member species

- [Conglomerate Species A](Conglomerate-Species-A): a cold-dwelling silicon lifeform that fights inside methane-filled bio-mechs
- Humanity, in the [EACO](Timeline-EACO) timeline

## At Earth

The Conglomerate attacked Earth without compromise, expecting another easy victory. It met a Remnant-Human Alliance whose joint technology had improved enormously, so it offered a compromise: join, help fight the greater threat, and give up Earth's resources. See [The Conglomerate War](Conglomerate-War).

## Human opinion

Late in the war, as humanity learns what the Conglomerate is fighting and where humanity fits in the grand scheme of things, a growing number of humans come to sympathise with its goals.
