**Type:** FTL network · **Used by:** [the Remnants](The-Remnants), [the Virulon](The-Virulon), [the Conglomerate](The-Conglomerate)

Stargate-like structures built from **wormholes**.

## Remnant and Virulon stargates

The Remnants and the Virulon each mastered stargate technology independently. Their gates are perfected and **very reliable**, but **expensive to build**, and they only connect fixed points.

## Conglomerate stargates

The Conglomerate took outdated stargate technology from the [Remnant homeworld](Remnant-Homeworld) and built **cheaper, more volatile copies**.

## Jumps through natural weak points

The Remnants can also jump through **existing wormholes and weak spots in space-time**. The bigger the ship, the harder the jump. Large ships need recharge time and the right position between jumps. The escape from the Remnant homeworld was an uncalculated jump of this kind.
