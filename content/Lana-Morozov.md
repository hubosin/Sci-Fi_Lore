**Role:** Link Program scientist; surrogate mother of [Series 3](Series-3-Clones) · **Species:** Human · **Died:** after [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault) · **Timeline:** Canon

A scientist on the original Link Program team, and the first and default surrogate mother of the Series 3 clones.

## Surrogacy

Lana was born without eggs and couldn't have children of her own, which was part of why she took on the role. As a continually willing subject, and to reduce variables, she became the default surrogate.

**Birth mother of:** [Adam](Adam), Carter, [Rose](Rose), [Olivia](Olivia), [Dean](Dean) and [Daniel](Daniel).

## The clones

Lana loved all the clones as her own children, even though she wasn't related to them, and that included the lab-grown ones. She saw the whole program as a found family and treated them all the same. She was deeply disheartened by the clones who died, especially those who survived birth and died later, but she kept working.

She struggled to balance her work with how she felt about them. Her greatest wish was for them to live normal lives. She was the main reason the clones were never sent on real field work, and part of why they were valued so highly.

## Death

Lana survived [Nina Lera](Nina-Lera)'s attempt on her life during [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault) but was left in shock. When she learned Daniel had died, she regretted surviving and blamed herself, believing that if she had died, things would never have escalated to his death. Already in a bad mental state, she was verbally attacked by [Mila](Mila), who blamed her for Nina's death. **Lana killed herself.**
