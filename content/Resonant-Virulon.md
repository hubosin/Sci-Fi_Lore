**Also called:** Virulon (B) · **Status:** at war with [the Virulon](The-Virulon) · **Timeline:** N/A

The Resonant Virulon are a tumour on the Virulon species: the result of a **failed augment** that breaks a Virulon's mind.

## Biology

A Resonant Virulon is a **cluster of flesh attached to a flat surface**. Nearby Virulon become part of that planet's **hivemind**. Like an immortal jellyfish, Resonant Virulon **pass memories down** instead of losing them.

In the rare case that the broken Virulon's mind stays whole, it becomes a **queen**, and the other Resonant Virulon on the planet become its **drones**.

## Status

The Virulon consider them **unholy**, and they are shot on sight. The two sides are in open **civil war**, and Resonant Virulon can take over entire forgotten systems such as the [Cygnus System](Cygnus-System).
