**Designation:** S35b · **Species:** Human ([Series 3 clone](Series-3-Clones)) · **Born:** surrogate ([Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov)) · **Role:** low-level tester, assistant to Adam · **Timeline:** Canon

The younger twin, born shortly after [Samantha](Samantha) from the same embryo. She shares Sam's designation, labelled by birth order.

## Appearance

* Long silver hair, pale skin and bright blue eyes
* Same height as Sam, about Neil's height; skinny
* **Label:** S35b, on her right shoulder
* Minimal linking implants on her upper back and neck

## Health

Born in worse health than Sam, though still a success:

* A faulty liver that slowly healed on its own as she grew
* **Sickle-cell disease** and poor circulation
* **Cancer** several times as a child, which she overcame each time
* An immune system weakened in ways similar to [Adam](Adam) and [Rose](Rose)

## The liver

When Sam's life was threatened, Olivia offered her own organs, believing Sam would fare better and that she would die in the process. Sam was strongly against it, but the procedure went ahead. Without Olivia knowing, she was given **Sam's rejected liver**, and her body accepted it.

## Personality

Shy and timid. She keeps her emotions hidden but is easily affected. Very adaptable, and willing to put herself on the line for others without a second thought.

## Role

Olivia does mech tests, but she isn't as competitive as Sam or Neil, so she's mostly used in smaller-scale tests, alongside [Mila](Mila). Otherwise she helps Adam with his work, usually pushing his wheelchair. She tried, without success, to break up the rivalry between Sam, Dean and Neil.
