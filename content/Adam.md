**Designation:** S31 · **Species:** Human ([Series 3 clone](Series-3-Clones)) · **Born:** surrogate ([Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov)) · **Role:** cloning scientist · **Timeline:** Canon

The first clone to survive birth and the first successful clone of [Series 3](Series-3-Clones). The other clones call him their eldest brother.

## Appearance

* Short, snow-white hair, very pale skin and red eyes
* Tall and very slim
* **Label:** S31, on his right wrist
* No linking implants. By the time they were developed he was long out of prototype testing.

## Health

As the first clone, Adam was heavily edited before anyone knew much about the process. He has **incredibly high aptitude** but extreme genetic conditions:

* Barely able to walk without consequence, and mostly uses a **wheelchair**
* Poor vision
* Weakened immune system
* Brittle bones and weak muscles

Actions most people consider ordinary cause him extreme pain.

## Personality

Incredibly intelligent, with **spirit** as his main virtue: ambitious, courageous and emotional. He believes in the cause and accepts that sacrifices have to be made for the greater good, but he opposes the clones' mistreatment and tries to rework the program from the inside to help his siblings.

## History

* Adam ran the early prototype tests until his health made it impossible. He planned at most to split testing 50/50 with [Neil](Neil), but once he thought Neil was old enough to handle it, Neil took over completely.
* He named every clone after their identification number, so none would be just a number.
* By the time the twins were made, Adam had joined the cloning program as a **scientist**. The governments were against it at first, but his knowledge and experience as a subject were too valuable. From then on, the clones' names were added to their labels.
* He was never as close to any sibling after [Rose](Rose)'s death.

## Relationships

* **[Rose](Rose):** his closest sibling
* **[Olivia](Olivia):** helps with his work and usually pushes his wheelchair
* **[Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov):** his birth mother
