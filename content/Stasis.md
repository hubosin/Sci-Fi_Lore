**Type:** suspended animation · **Used by:** [the Conglomerate](The-Conglomerate)

Long-term suspended animation used to store soldiers between deployments.

## Facilities

The Conglomerate keeps its stasis facilities **deep underground**. A supervisor oversees the soldiers held there.

## Transport

Soldiers are carried between worlds in stasis aboard large, mostly empty transport ships that travel through wormholes. Waking means pulling out the tubes and needles that run into the skin. Malfunctions can wake a sleeper early.

## Human use

In the [EACO](Timeline-EACO) timeline, human pilots who join the Conglomerate are put into stasis soon after the war and woken an unknown time later for deployment.
