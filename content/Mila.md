**Designation:** S38 · **Species:** Human ([Series 3 clone](Series-3-Clones)) · **Born:** surrogate ([Nina Lera](Nina-Lera)) · **Role:** low-level tester · **Timeline:** Canon

The first **"perfect"** clone, and the first born to a surrogate other than [Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov).

## Appearance

* Long, faded blonde hair in pigtails, deep blue eyes
* Short
* **Label:** S38, on her right shoulder
* Linking implants on her upper back, neck and the back of her head, more refined than [Nova](Nova)'s

## Upbringing

Because of her importance to the program, Mila was treated like a princess while the other clones were treated as less important. Things went back to normal once she was old enough for testing, when her importance became a drawback: no one would risk losing her in advanced tests, so she was only allowed to do low-level tests with [Olivia](Olivia).

## Personality

Spoiled by her upbringing, and most people find her annoying. She used to see herself as a miracle saviour of the others. She does care about her siblings, but hides it by acting mean or distant. As she grew older, alongside [Daniel](Daniel), she became more humble.

## Relationships

* **[Nova](Nova):** dislikes her because Nova is more skilled and gets the advanced tests, not knowing that's because Nova is considered more expendable
* **[Daniel](Daniel):** close friend, who did for her what [Rose](Rose) did for Adam
* **[Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov):** Lana treated her as a daughter too, but Mila never saw her as a mother in the slightest
* **[Nina Lera](Nina-Lera):** her surrogate mother
* **[Samantha](Samantha):** frequent clashes

## After BRIDGE-Fault

After Daniel's death in [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault), Mila went back to her old behaviour to shield her emotions. In denial that Nina was the one in the wrong, she **blamed Lana for Nina's death** and attacked her verbally, shortly before Lana killed herself. After Lana's death and the others' reactions, Mila spent a long time out of character: staring blankly into space, depressed and empty, doing nothing.
