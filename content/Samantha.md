**Designation:** S35a · **Also called:** Sam · **Species:** Human ([Series 3 clone](Series-3-Clones)) · **Born:** lab-grown · **Role:** prototype tester · **Timeline:** Canon

The elder of the twins. She and [Olivia](Olivia) were made from the **same embryo and genetic code** to test whether lab-grown clones justify the extra cost over surrogate-born ones. Sam was the lab baby.

## Appearance

* Short silver hair, pale skin and bright blue eyes
* Same height as Olivia, about Neil's height
* Skinny, but stronger than Olivia from working out with [Neil](Neil)
* **Label:** S35a, on her right shoulder. The twins were the first clones with names on their labels.
* Minimal linking implants on her upper back and neck

## Health

The healthier twin, though born with a **liver condition** and allergies. Her first liver transplant was rejected and she came close to death. Olivia offered her own organs, and Sam was strongly against it, but the second procedure went ahead and Sam recovered.

## Personality

Competitive in a friendly way, jokey, very blunt, tomboyish and rowdy.

## Role

After recovering she joined [Neil](Neil) as one of the two main prototype testers.

## Relationships

* **[Olivia](Olivia) and [Nova](Nova):** very protective of both
* **[Neil](Neil):** frequent clashes from their rivalry
* **[Mila](Mila):** butts heads with her over her high-and-mighty attitude
* **[Dean](Dean):** partner in mischief
