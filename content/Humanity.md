**Homeworld:** [Earth](Earth) · **Tech rating:** 4.5 in the real world ([scale](Technology#species-tech-ratings)) · **Timeline:** Canon

## Society

Human society in this universe still looks like the **1980s**. After first contact the whole world was pointed at developing joint technology with the Remnants and preparing for the Conglomerate. Daily life, cars, homes and consumer technology stayed much the same, while **military technology, weapons, spaceflight and power generation** leapt ahead.

## The science race

The Cold War never became an arms race. The U.S. and USSR competed on science and spaceflight instead: see [The Science Race](Science-Race). Weapons were still developed, often in secret, and those classified advances matter during [First Contact](First-Contact) in the [I-Line](Timeline-I-Line). By 1984 humanity had fusion power, the space warship USS *Independence*, and crewed landings on Mars. See [Canon Timeline](Timeline-Canon).

## Factions

- **Alliance bloc:** mainly the Soviet Bloc and NATO, plus other assorted nations. Signed the [Remnant-Human Alliance](Remnant-Human-Alliance).
- **[Opposition bloc](Opposition-Bloc):** countries that left NATO and countries opposed to the alliance. It includes people who distrust the Remnants, people who deny they exist, and later people who sympathise with the Conglomerate.

## Military

- [First-generation mechs](First-Generation-Mechs), and later [neural-linked bio-tech mechs](Later-Generation-Mechs)
- Air forces flying VTOL and conventional aircraft
- Space warships from 1976 on, starting with the [USS *Independence*](USS-Independence)
- [Laser weapons](Laser-Weapons) at realistic power levels

## Power

Fusion and solar for spacecraft, fusion and wind on planets, and radioisotope thermoelectric generators for specialised craft. Large installations such as the [Northern Facility City](Northern-Facility-City) run on hidden nuclear reactors.

## Human faster-than-light travel

Humanity uses the [Alcubierre drive](Alcubierre-Drive).
