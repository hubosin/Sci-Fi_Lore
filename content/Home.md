In 1984 a probe orbiting Europa spots an alien fleet. It belongs to **the Remnants**, the last survivors of a species whose homeworld was stripped bare by **the Conglomerate**, an alliance of alien species that takes whatever resources it needs to fight something even worse. Humanity, still locked in a science race that never ended, takes the refugees in. Together they have to face the Conglomerate when it comes for Earth.
