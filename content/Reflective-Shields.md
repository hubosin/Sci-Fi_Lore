**Type:** defensive system · **Timeline:** Canon

Shields built from materials that reflect nearly 100% of incoming energy.

## Design

The shield's surfaces are angled so that **steep faces point toward the enemy**, deflecting beams and projectiles. Only a head-on hit gets through.

## Energy shields

Energy-based versions can draw extra power to **vaporise incoming physical projectiles**.

## Remnant shields

[The Remnants](The-Remnants) had basic reflective energy shields at first contact.
