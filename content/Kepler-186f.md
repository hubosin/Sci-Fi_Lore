**Virulon name:** Adecron (*Add-Eh-Cur-On*) · **Species:** [the Virulon](The-Virulon)

The homeworld of the Virulon.

## Environment

Its conditions gave the Virulon their high temperature resistance and their ability to store large amounts of oxygen.

## History

About **15,000 years** before contact with humanity, the Virulon lived here in small rural communities. **Overmining and extreme industrialisation** drove them off the planet.

## Today

Kepler-186f remains the seat of Virulon power:

- The monarch rules from here.
- Young Virulon come here to be tested and augmented.
- New foremen are chosen here.
- Their siblings are sealed inside the **orbiting signal amplifiers**.
- Remote systems send a **tithe to Adecron** once a century.
