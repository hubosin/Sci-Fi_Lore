**Type:** war front · **Location:** [Earth](Earth) · **Timeline:** [RHV](Timeline-RHV)

A forested region **rich in minerals and oil** that became a front of **unconventional warfare** during the [Conglomerate War](Conglomerate-War).

## The Conglomerate landing

Conglomerate ground forces landed **north of Denali National Park** and prospected the region for resources. Near the end of the Conglomerate presence on Earth, a large force landed and began mining, burning the forests around it.

## The defence

- **U.S. forces** fought a guerrilla war on the ground and flew VTOL aircraft from **covert forest bases**.
- **Soviet forces** operated hidden airbases and patrols near the Bering Strait.
- A combined **US-Soviet** strike eventually destroyed the Conglomerate mining operation, devastating the landscape around it.
