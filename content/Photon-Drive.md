**Type:** radiation-pressure propulsion · **See also:** [Propulsion](Propulsion)

Photons have no mass but still exert force through radiation pressure. A photon drive is essentially a **giant space flashlight**.

## Strengths

- Needs only electricity. Attach a nuclear reactor and it has **unlimited propellant** as long as the reactor has fuel.
- Very easy to fuel.
- Well suited to slower-than-light **interstellar** travel.

## Weaknesses

- Tiny thrust. Changing speed takes a very long time.
