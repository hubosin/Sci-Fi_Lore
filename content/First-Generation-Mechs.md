**Also called:** Gen 1 · **Builder:** [Humanity](Humanity) · **Designed with:** [the Remnants](The-Remnants) · **Timeline:** Canon

The first human war mechs, made of **pure technology** with no organic parts.

## Design

- Designed jointly by humans and Remnants and built by humans during the [Second Industrial Age](Second-Industrial-Age)
- Look like **military vehicles**: practical and optimised for battle rather than humanoid
- Built solely for combat

## Crew

Each needs **several pilots**. Crews are extensively trained, but technically anyone can pilot one.

## Service

- Carried the fighting through the [Conglomerate War](Conglomerate-War). **Most first-generation pilots died** in the war.
- **[RHV](Timeline-RHV):** decommissioned after the war, and surviving pilots honourably discharged.

## Immunity

With no organic parts, Gen 1 mechs are **immune** to [the Ultimate Threat](The-disease). That makes them valuable again during [the Outbreak](The-Outbreak), when veteran pilots return to them.
