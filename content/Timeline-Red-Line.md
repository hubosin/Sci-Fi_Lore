## Events

1. After contact is made, the human fleet attacks the Remnant fleet (or the bomb of the [I-Line](Timeline-I-Line) reaches it), and **the Remnants go extinct**.
2. Without the alliance, the Conglomerate judges humanity **unfit to join**. Earth is rich in resources, so the Conglomerate goes to war. Humanity's only real chance is escape.
3. **Earth loses.** Humanity is wiped out and Earth's resources are taken.

The same ending is reached from Canon if the Remnant-Human Alliance loses the war after refusing the compromise.
