**Type:** FTL method · **See also:** [Stargates](Stargates), [FTL](FTL)

A way of making a **temporary wormhole** to any location.

## How it works

1. A wormhole is a **black hole** at the entry point and a **white hole** at the exit.
2. The black hole rips through space-time, creating an infinitely dense point.
3. To exit, a second black hole is created where the first one pierces through space-time.
4. **Quantum bouncing** turns the pair into a very short-lived wormhole.

## Exotic matter

A white hole collapses back into a black hole if any matter touches it. A small amount of **exotic matter** keeps the wormhole open for a short time. Exotic matter needs **negative mass**, which is made by cooling an element to near absolute zero and using a very small laser to reverse the rotation of its ions.
