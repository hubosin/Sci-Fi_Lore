**Type:** sabotage and attack · **Location:** a BRIDGE facility · **Perpetrator:** [Nina Lera](Nina-Lera) · **Timeline:** Canon

**BRIDGE-Fault** was an attack on the [BRIDGE Project](BRIDGE-Project) from inside, using the repaired [G2M01](BRIDGE-Mechs#lpg2-m01). It killed [Daniel](Daniel) (S39) and Nina Lera, ended [Series 3](Series-3-Clones), and led to the death of [Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov).

## Background

[Nina Lera](Nina-Lera), the second Series 3 surrogate, gave birth to [Mila](Mila), the first "perfect" clone, and believed this made her essential and Lana redundant. When Lana gave birth to Daniel, another perfect clone, it proved the process worked with **any** surrogate. Nina was the one who was now unimportant, and she came to hate both Daniel and Lana.

## The attack

1. Daniel was told he would be testing one of the earlier prototypes. He was put into a **modified [LPG1 M01](BRIDGE-Mechs#lpg1-m01)** exosuit.
2. Nina drove him into the cockpit of the repaired **G2M01** and boarded with him, but did not link to the mech herself.
3. Daniel, still a child, was **linked to G2M01**. Nina wore G1M01's control helmet, which let her control the exosuit and so control Daniel's movements.
4. **Explosives** were packed into the already cramped two-person cockpit and mounted on the mech's exterior.
5. The attack targeted BRIDGE itself and included an **attempt on Lana's life**, which she survived.

## The fight

[Nova](Nova) was sent out in the Generation 3 test prototype **[BG3 M00](BRIDGE-Mechs#bg3-m00-p)**, a mech with no weapons or advanced armour, to stop G2M01.

Inside G2M01, Daniel suffered severe mental strain and **brain damage** from being connected to such a powerful mech so young. Even so, despite Nina's control, he managed to **shut G2M01 down**.

Nina lashed out and **detonated the explosives**, trying to do as much damage to BRIDGE as she could. Nova prevented any further deaths. Only the two people inside G2M01 died: Daniel and Nina.

## Aftermath

* **Lana Morozov** was in shock after the attempt on her life. When she learned of Daniel's death she regretted surviving, and blamed herself, believing that if she had died things would never have escalated to Daniel's death. Already in a bad mental state, she was verbally attacked by Mila, who blamed her for Nina's death. **Lana killed herself.**
* **Mila** went back to her old cold, spoiled behaviour to shield herself after Daniel's death. After Lana's death, and seeing how the others reacted, she spent a long time blank and depressed.
* **Series 3 ended.** BRIDGE moved on to [Series 4](Pilot-Series#series-4).
