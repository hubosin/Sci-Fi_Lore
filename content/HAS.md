**Full name:** Humanity Against Sovereignty · **Also called:** Human Independence · **Type:** human resistance movement · **Timeline:** Canon / [RHV](Timeline-RHV)

A human movement that opposes governments, militarisation and, eventually, both the alliance and the Conglomerate.

## Overview

HAS began as a small group fighting what it saw as sovereign governments, and slowly grew into an organisation broadly opposed to [the Remnant-Human Alliance](Remnant-Human-Alliance). It also opposes [the Conglomerate](The-Conglomerate), acting as a **third party** that shakes things up.

## Origins

HAS grew out of the distrust that followed [First Contact](First-Contact). Its early supporters included:

* **Deniers**, who didn't believe the Remnants existed and thought the military was using them as an excuse to overwork and underpay civilians for mass weapons manufacturing aimed at the USSR
* **Distrusters**, who feared the Remnants were manipulating humanity to get its weapons technology
* Later, **Conglomerate sympathisers**, as humanity came to understand the Conglomerate's goals and humanity's place in the grand scheme of things. This view was most common near the end of the war.

See also [Opposition Bloc](Opposition-Bloc).

## History

### BRIDGE

The huge number of disappearances during [Series 2](Pilot-Series#series-2) testing is what first drew HAS to the [BRIDGE Project](BRIDGE-Project). BRIDGE publicly claimed to be a philanthropy project building prosthetics and restoration mechs, but HAS suspected, correctly, that it was developing advanced weapons. HAS sent people to investigate and shut down any attempt at weaponisation. Their orders were to halt the program by **destroying or damaging mechs, technology and blueprints**.

### The mercenaries

Two mercenaries hired by HAS went beyond those orders and decided to kill BRIDGE's pilots instead. They tracked the most frequently used pilot, [Nova](Nova), and cornered her in an alley intending to assault and kill her. They didn't know she was armed and skilled enough to kill them both, which she did.

### After the war

HAS keeps fighting government programs and weaponisation after the war, **scrapping old mechs** and sustaining a large portion of humanity's survivors.
