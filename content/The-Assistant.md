**Name:** not yet decided · **Role:** Link Program engineer · **Later:** head of Generation 5 linking tech · **Timeline:** Canon

Originally a brand-new engineering intern who had just joined the Link Program.

## Role

One of the two people [the Engineer](The-Engineer) shared their findings and plans for the G2M01 link system with directly. After the Engineer's death, the Assistant was the only person who could decipher their notes.

Like the rest of the Link Program staff, the Assistant was forced to stay on after the government takeover. Because of their early knowledge of the device, they eventually headed development of **[Generation 5](BRIDGE-Mechs#generation-5) linking tech**.
