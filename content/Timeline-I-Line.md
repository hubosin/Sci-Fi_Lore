In the I-Line, first contact goes worse before it goes better. Everything before the bomb launch, and everything after the Remnant fleet reaches Mars, is the same as [Canon](Timeline-Canon).

## Events

1. After the Remnant scout convoy is destroyed at Earth, a **small automated probe carrying an atomic bomb** is launched at the main Remnant ship. It is slow next to Remnant craft but practically undetectable. Destroying the main ship would set off a chain reaction pulling the rest of the fleet into Jupiter.
2. The large Remnant ships can't escape. They are recharging after their jump into the solar system, and big ships need time and the right position between jumps.
3. Because human spacecraft are slow, the bomb takes a long time to reach the fleet, with the whole affair lasting **up to two years** after first contact. Meanwhile the fleet stays near Jupiter harvesting its moons. Humans translate Remnant transmissions, and the Remnants attempt surrender and then propose an alliance, exchanging history, science, technology and language.
4. Humanity has no ship fast enough to catch the bomb, and the Remnants can neither find nor disarm it. The Remnants send plans for a **small, fast ship**, and a crew is drawn from the major spacefaring nations (US, USSR and others).
5. The crew reaches the bomb, and one of them sacrifices themself to **detonate it early**, saving the Remnant fleet.
6. The surviving crew escorts the Remnant fleet to its holding pattern over Mars, and history rejoins **Canon**.
