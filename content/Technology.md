## Species tech ratings

Species are rated from **1** (pre-industrial) to **10** (FTL-capable). Real-world humanity scores **4.5**.

## Power and production

| Faction | Power sources |
|---|---|
| [The Conglomerate](The-Conglomerate) | Dyson spheres, captured technology |
| [The Virulon](The-Virulon) | Fusion reactors on massive space installations and surface bases, fission reactors in smaller craft, crystal/nuclear diamond batteries for storage |
| [Humanity](Humanity) | Fusion and solar for spacecraft, fusion and wind on planets, radioisotope thermoelectric generators for specialised craft |

**Fusion** is mainly used in large, stationary installations. It's possible in something as small as a mech, but **fission** is the better fit there.
