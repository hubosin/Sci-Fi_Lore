**Type:** star system · **Controlled by:** [the Virulon](The-Virulon) · **Capital:** Ciroth

A small, unimportant Virulon star system **hundreds of light-years past Kepler-186f**, in the region humans call **Cygnus**.

## Isolation

It is left to sustain itself. Its only contact with the greater Virulon and their homeworld is a convoy **once a century** that collects the **tithe to Adecron**. Despite this it is thriving and constantly expanding.

## Ciroth

The system's capital and industrial hub, devoted entirely to **research and development of bio-augments**.

## Threat

Forgotten systems like this one are vulnerable to takeover by the [Resonant Virulon](Resonant-Virulon).
