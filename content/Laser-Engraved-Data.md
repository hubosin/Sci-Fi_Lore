**Type:** information storage · **Timeline:** Canon

Information is engraved at extremely small scale into a material such as plastic and read by shining laser light through it.

## Uses

It's used for information that is better **seen** than stored as raw data:

- Books
- Blueprints
- Training manuals, such as those for [mech](Mechs) operation
- Legal documents

## Readers

Automated readers have a built-in laser and a slot for the engraved material. As the reader moves the laser, different images appear, so a single engraving can hold **animations** or multiple views, such as a rotating blueprint. Everything is stored in the engraving itself; the reader only moves the light.
