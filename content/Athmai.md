**Full name:** Athmai Orbital Dropped Fortification · **Type:** space-launched drop pod / fortification · **Manufacturer:** [the Virulon](The-Virulon)

![Athmai drop pod](images/athmai-drop-pod.png)

A multipurpose drop pod that becomes a fortified base as soon as it lands.

## Variants

| Variant | Capacity |
|---|---|
| **Mounted turret** | A large plasma cannon, artillery piece or machine gun mounted on top |
| **Troop transport** | Large cargo bay and extra small arms. Fortification and supplies for **350 troops** and **10 medium tanks** |
| **Heavy transport** | Troop housing replaced by a very large cargo bay and a small helipad. Carries **50 small/medium mechs**, **20 large mechs**, and **10 helicopters or VTOL aircraft** |

**Command option:** any variant can swap part of its space for a **command centre**, allowing command from the front line.

## Endurance

- Supplies for its troops and vehicles for just over **two months** without resupply
- All variants can be **resupplied from the air** with ammunition, batteries, food and new troops

## Power

It generates power only from **solar**, but deploys with a **diamond crystal battery** that runs the station for well over **six months** of normal use.

## Deployment

1. Dropped from the bottom of a mothership in orbit
2. Plummets through the atmosphere, steered by an assortment of flaps
3. Lands at a designated point on the battlefield
4. Its **four legs slam into the ground** and bury themselves deep. The Athmai is almost impossible to move unless the legs are lifted.
