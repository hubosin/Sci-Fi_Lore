**Name:** not yet decided · **Role:** surgeon; developer of the link implant procedure · **Timeline:** Canon

One of the two people [the Engineer](The-Engineer) shared their plans for the G2M01 link system with directly.

## The procedure

The Doctor worked with the Engineer on the logistics of the direct link and how it would connect, and with a partner secretly performed the surgery on the Engineer. They then turned it into **clear, replicable plans** so the G2M01 link could be fitted to other pilots.

## Under BRIDGE

After the government takeover, the Doctor and their partner were the only people allowed to operate on "high-quality" subjects, because they knew the procedure best. They were phased out of the program after [Series 3](Series-3-Clones).

The Doctor performed [Nova](Nova)'s linking implant surgery.
