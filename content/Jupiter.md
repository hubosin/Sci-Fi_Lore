**Type:** gas giant · **Timeline:** Canon

The planet where the **Remnant fleet** arrived after its jump.

## Remnant presence

The fleet settled near Jupiter to recharge and to **harvest resources from its moons**, pulling some of them unusually close. If the main Remnant ship had been destroyed there, the chain reaction would have dragged the whole fleet into Jupiter's gravity ([I-Line](Timeline-I-Line)).

## Europa

The human probe [*Aurora 2*](Aurora-2), orbiting Europa, first sighted the fleet on September 8, 1984.
