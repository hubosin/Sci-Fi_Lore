**Type:** military facility city · **Location:** a mountain in a far-north frozen desert, [Earth](Earth) · **Timeline:** Canon / [RHV](Timeline-RHV)

A militarised city that also houses a civilian population. It is home to [Nova](Nova).

## Layout

- Large, rectangular buildings with few windows, placed on a high, almost unlivable vantage point
- Residential blocks of small two-room units opening onto long shared balconies that overlook the city
- A **military hospital**, reached through military-base corridors
- A **mech testing hangar** with control rooms overlooking the testing bay

## Climate

Constantly overcast and grey. Everything is covered in a thin, dusty sheet of frost, and the diffuse light seems to come from everywhere at once.

## Power

Civilians believe the city's heat comes from **geothermal** sources. In fact **large water-cooled nuclear reactors** deep below the facility power it, and their waste heat is piped up for heating and hot water. The reactors and their true purpose are kept secret from the civilian population.

## Events

* The two HAS mercenaries who attacked [Nova](Nova) cornered her in an alley on her way to the military hospital. Police tape and bloodstains in the snow marked the spot for a day afterwards.
* Nova's [implant surgery](Nova#implant-surgery) took place in the city's military hospital, and her first bio-tech mech boot-up in the testing hangar.

## Daily life

Residents wear heavy, near-identical grey winter clothing and mostly move in groups. Living units provide standard-issue clothing and simple meals from wall-mounted ovens.
