**Designation:** S34 · **Species:** Human ([Series 3 clone](Series-3-Clones)) · **Born:** lab-grown · **Role:** main prototype tester · **Timeline:** Canon

The second successful clone and the first grown in an **artificial womb**.

## Appearance

* Short white hair with a bluish grey/silver tint, less stark than Adam's
* Red eyes, usually hidden
* Big but not tall. One of the two clones with real build, because he works hard at getting stronger
* **Label:** S34, on his right wrist
* Minimal linking implants on his back and neck, added much later than the others'

## Health

Neil has far fewer edits than [Adam](Adam) but still high aptitude, and is much healthier.

* **Blind**, relying on his other senses, which proved very useful in feedback tests
* **Nerve damage:** he can't feel pain in some areas and can't tell hot from cold

## Personality

Passionate and a fighter. Hotheaded, but he would do anything to protect his ideals and the people he loves, and he won't compromise. Good-natured but oblivious, and he doesn't know the full truth behind the project.

## Role

Neil took over **all** prototype testing from Adam, whose health was suffering badly from it. [Samantha](Samantha) later joined him as one of the two main testers.

## Relationships

* **[Samantha](Samantha):** friendly rival and workout partner
* **[Dean](Dean):** often teamed up with Sam against him
