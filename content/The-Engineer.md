**Name:** not yet decided · **Role:** Link Program engineer; inventor of the direct link · **Pilot series:** [Series 1](Pilot-Series#series-1) · **Died:** piloting [G2M01](BRIDGE-Mechs#lpg2-m01) · **Timeline:** Canon

The Link Program engineer who invented the **direct-link system** and co-developed the surgery that connects it.

## The direct link

The Engineer designed G2M01's link, which connects straight to the pilot's brain. They trained two people on it: their [assistant](The-Assistant), and [the Doctor](The-Doctor), who worked with them on the logistics of the device and how it would connect.

## Death

The Engineer volunteered as G2M01's first pilot. The two team doctors secretly performed the implant surgery. After a few check-ups the Engineer entered the cockpit and a scientist outside powered it on.

A power fluctuation overrode the limits meant to protect the pilot. In pain and mental overload, the Engineer involuntarily drove G2M01 through the lab, destroying equipment, though no one outside the mech died. They were found dead in the cockpit after an exterior shutdown. Later examination found severe damage to the **prefrontal cortex**.

## Legacy

The Engineer left behind blueprints and research for G2M01's link, but it was experimental and mostly illegible. Only the Assistant could decipher it. The Doctor knew mainly the procedure and had made their own replicable notes.

Later testing found the Engineer, like the rest of Series 1, had only **average aptitude**.
