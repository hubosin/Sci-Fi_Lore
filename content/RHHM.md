**Full name:** Remnant-Human Habitat Maker · **Timeline:** Canon

One of the first devices built by the [Remnant-Human Alliance](Remnant-Human-Alliance). It's a large machine that accelerates plant growth in a field around it.

## Function

- It affects organisms within its field, and is designed to affect only **plants**.
- It speeds up growth and gathers its own water.
- The field radius depends on the model and the power put in.
- The effect falls off steeply at the edge of the field.

## Limits

An RHHM can't be activated where conditions are too harsh: no existing life, extreme heat or cold, or no material to grow from. Without nutrients and sunlight, nothing grows. RHHMs are hard to set up and hard to remove, but most consider them worth it.

## History

- **Origin:** based on devices the Remnants used to improve the lacklustre plant life of their homeworld. On Earth they helped adapt it to optimal human conditions to strengthen the new alliance.
- **Before the war:** a philanthropy project to revitalise places with harsh conditions.
- **During the war:** after the second Conglomerate attack destroyed most of the world's forests, RHHMs were **supercharged** in habitable areas to grow dense cover. The alliance hid its smaller weapons in these forests and ambushed Conglomerate forces from them.
- **After the war ([RHV](Timeline-RHV)):** the last supercharged RHHM created [The Ring](The-Ring). Smaller RHHMs elsewhere support survivor settlements.
