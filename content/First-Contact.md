**Timeline:** Canon · **Date:** September–November 1984

First contact between humanity and [the Remnants](The-Remnants). It took about two months to go from sighting an unknown fleet to escorting it to Mars.

## Background

The Remnants had just escaped the destruction of their homeworld by [the Conglomerate](The-Conglomerate). Their evacuation fleet made an uncalculated [jump](FTL) and came out near our solar system, where Earth was the closest habitable planet. The fleet settled near [Jupiter](Jupiter) to harvest resources from its moons and recharge. The largest Remnant ships need time and the right position between jumps.

## Sighting

On **September 8, 1984**, the Europa probe [*Aurora 2*](Aurora-2) sighted the Remnant fleet. Five days later its trajectory was mapped, and U.S. and Soviet warships were deployed to intercept and destroy it.

## The scout convoy

On **September 24** a Remnant scout convoy reached Earth undetected and landed, unannounced, in what Earth's militaries saw as enemy territory. Earth's forces attacked and destroyed it. Remnant weapons were no better than human ones at the time, so the fight was short. The Remnants surrendered almost immediately and casualties were low.

## Contact

Radio contact with the main Remnant force was established on **September 27**. By **October 4** the Remnant had worked out English, and real communication began. The Remnants attempted surrender, then proposed an alliance, exchanging history, science, technology and language. They also warned humanity about the Conglomerate.

## Escort to Mars

On **October 5** the U.S. and USSR retasked their warships from intercept to escort. The fleets met in the inner asteroid belt on **October 17**, and the Remnant fleet reached a holding pattern over [Mars](Mars) on **October 29**. A diplomatic squadron reached Earth on **November 11**, and on **November 20** the [Remnant monarch](Remnant-Monarch) and elected officials addressed the U.N.

## Public reaction

Not everyone believed it. Some people thought the Remnants were a government invention, an excuse to overwork and underpay civilians for mass weapons manufacturing aimed at the other bloc. Others believed the aliens were real but manipulating humanity to get its weapons technology. These fears peaked in the mass protests of **May 20, 1985**. See [Opposition Bloc](Opposition-Bloc).
