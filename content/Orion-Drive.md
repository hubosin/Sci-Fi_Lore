**Type:** nuclear pulse propulsion · **See also:** [Propulsion](Propulsion)

Instead of burning fuel, the craft throws **nuclear bombs** behind it and rides the shockwaves.

## Requirements

- Heavy **radiation shielding** at the rear of the craft
- **Shock-absorbing** equipment at the rear of the craft

## Efficiency

Much more efficient than chemical propulsion.

## Limits

It can't be used in low Earth orbit without frying satellites and irradiating the atmosphere. It is practical beyond the Moon and in interplanetary space.
