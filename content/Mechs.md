|  | [First-Generation Mechs](First-Generation-Mechs) | [Later-Generation Mechs](Later-Generation-Mechs) |

| **Origin** | Pure technology, designed jointly by humans and Remnants | Bio-organic. Late-war designs use bio-tech stolen from the Conglomerate |

## Development

The first mechs were designed by humans and Remnants together and built by humans as pure technology. They needed several pilots and looked like military vehicles. After the alliance captured Conglomerate technology, the secret [BRIDGE Project](BRIDGE-Project) reverse-engineered its neural link, leading through a series of prototypes ([BRIDGE Mechs](BRIDGE-Mechs)) to the humanoid, single-pilot later-generation mechs. Later generations need pilots with specific genes, and the program went as far as cloning them ([Series 3](Series-3-Clones)).
