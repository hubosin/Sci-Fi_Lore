**Homeworld:** Kepler-186f ("Adecron") · **Tech rating:** 7 ([scale](Technology#species-tech-ratings)) · **Government:** absolute monarchy · **Timeline:** Canon

A carbon-based species that remade itself through bioengineering and rules an empire of enslaved species. The Virulon are one species split by a civil war with the [Resonant Virulon](Resonant-Virulon).

## Biology

The Virulon come from [Kepler-186f](Kepler-186f), called **Adecron** (*Add-Eh-Cur-On*) in their language. They store large amounts of oxygen in their bodies and resist high temperatures, thanks both to their homeworld and to biological enhancement.

Thousands of years of bioengineering have left them looking less like their original species and more like **fleshy abominations**: enhanced senses, extra or modified limbs, and extreme mental capacity.

- **Lifespan:** 100–120 human years (300+ Kepler-186f years)
- **Offspring:** usually only one or two
- **Population:** fewer than 1,000 true Virulon per planet. Their numbers are tiny for their civilisation's size, but their industrial capacity and armies are huge because they are built from slaves.

## Bioengineering

Every young Virulon goes through an extreme set of tests on the homeworld to decide which biological and mechanical improvements they need. Each is then assigned a job by their planet's foreman and receives more augments suited to it:

| Role | Typical augments |
|---|---|
| Commanders | Improved senses, extra or better organs |
| Starship pilots | Extra limbs, larger lungs, better temperature resistance for space exposure |
| All Virulon | Stronger immune systems, strength, vastly improved mental capacity |

### Slaves

The Virulon can make limited copies of other species through genetic modification and can **enslave entire populations** through bioengineering. Slave races are augmented for their purpose: factory workers get strength, extra limbs or temperature resistance, and soldiers get varying strength, mental capacity and limbs. **Every slave** gets a [mental inhibitor](Mental-Inhibitor) implanted in the spine (or equivalent), which severs the connection to the brain and controls all movement. Over time the body absorbs the implant, and it can't be removed without killing the host.

## Government

A single **monarch** rules the whole species. Each planet's workforce and army is controlled by one **foreman**, whose sole purpose is keeping it in check.

When a foreman dies, the young of that generation travel to the homeworld. One of them receives the **blessing of the monarch**: a neural implant linked to every other foreman and planet through massive planetary arrays. Afterwards the rest are taken back to the homeworld and **sealed inside an orbiting signal amplifier**.

## History

- **~15,000 years before first contact with humanity:** the Virulon live in small rural communities on one planet.
- **Industrial collapse:** overmining and extreme industrialisation destroy the homeworld's habitability and force them off it. The exodus demands adaptation faster than evolution allows, which drives bioengineering.
- **Rise of the monarch:** after colonising their home system, the monarch uses bioengineered adaptations to take control of the entire species and drives mass expansion across many star systems.
- **Civil war:** failed augments give rise to the [Resonant Virulon](Resonant-Virulon).
- **Contact with Earth:** the Virulon arrive in the solar system and meet the [Remnant-Human Alliance](Remnant-Human-Alliance). They sign a treaty and make limited technology trades.

## Remote systems

Outlying systems are left to sustain themselves, and their only contact with the homeworld is a convoy **once a century** to collect a **tithe to Adecron**. One is the [Cygnus System](Cygnus-System), whose capital **Ciroth** is devoted to bio-augment research. Virulon space outside Resonant control is referred to as **Ordinal**.

## Weaknesses

- Very small population
- Implants can be damaged or destroyed by large amounts of **radiation** or **electrical charge**
- Inflexible: it can take tens to hundreds of years to produce soldiers with specific augments or new technology

## Technology

- **Power:** fusion reactors on massive space installations and surface bases, fission reactors in smaller craft, and crystal/nuclear diamond batteries for storage
- **FTL:** perfected [stargates](Stargates) built from wormholes
- **Military:** the [Athmai](Athmai) orbital drop fortification
