**Also called:** bio-tech mechs · **Builder:** [Humanity](Humanity) · **Timeline:** Canon (late war onward)

Humanoid, bio-organic mechs neural-linked to a single pilot. Late-war designs use bio-technology **stolen from the Conglomerate**.

## Design

- **Organic or organic-integrated** construction
- **Humanoid**, with human proportions, hands, fingers and opposable thumbs, because a human-shaped machine links better with a human mind
- Look like classic mechs

## Pilots

Each mech has **one pilot**, who needs specific genes and is synced to a specific machine. Candidates are chosen for **affinity**, and some are raised for the role from childhood. [Nova](Nova) was one of the first successful pilots.

## Neural-link implants

Pilots receive surgically implanted **metal ports** in the lower neck and upper back.

## Boarding sequence

1. The pilot is decontaminated and dressed in a tight black flight suit that fits around the implants.
2. The pilot walks a darkened gantry into a tiny cockpit shaped around the seat. Feet lock into holds, and hands go into glove-like controls on beams behind the seat.
3. Technicians plug cords into each implant, and a bar locks over the pilot's abdomen.
4. The pilot flips a covered switch and the cockpit **floods with a cold, oxygen-rich fluid**, which the pilot has to breathe in.
5. The mech moves with the pilot's brain signals.

Inside, the pilot sees the world through a heavy, simplified **HUD** built for combat efficiency.

## Vulnerability

Later-generation mechs are **vulnerable** to [the Ultimate Threat](The-disease). Infected mechs mutate, trap their pilots, and eventually fuse with them. See [The Outbreak](The-Outbreak).

## By timeline

- **[RHV](Timeline-RHV):** kept in service after the war for clean-up and reconstruction, until the Outbreak turns them into monsters.
- **[EACO](Timeline-EACO):** the Conglomerate's front-line infantry, each a single soldier with the power of a tank.
