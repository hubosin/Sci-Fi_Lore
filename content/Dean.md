**Designation:** S36 · **Species:** Human ([Series 3 clone](Series-3-Clones)) · **Born:** surrogate ([Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov)) · **Died:** four months before age 6 · **Timeline:** Canon

## Appearance

* Medium-length spiky white hair and blue eyes
* Short even for his age; he would have been a late bloomer
* **Label:** S36, on his right shoulder

## Health and death

Dean's condition was very similar to [Adam](Adam)'s, combined with unresponsive nerves like [Neil](Neil)'s. Because his pain receptors didn't work, he suffered **several bone fractures without knowing**. He died in his sleep of **internal bleeding**, four months before his sixth birthday.

He never got to test a prototype, but his aptitude would supposedly have been high, similar to Adam's.

## Personality

Always full of energy, and a troublemaker.

## Relationships

* **[Samantha](Samantha):** his partner in mischief. The two usually teamed up against [Neil](Neil), while [Olivia](Olivia) tried to break up the rivalry and Adam stood ready to step in if it ever went past fun. It never did.
