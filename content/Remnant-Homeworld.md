**Type:** planet (destroyed) · **Species:** [the Remnants](The-Remnants)

## Environment

**Rich in resources**, which is why it was targeted, but home to **lacklustre plant life**. The Remnants built devices to improve it, and these became the basis of the [RHHM](RHHM).

## Destruction

The Conglomerate judged the Remnants unfit to join, exterminated the population, and **strip-mined the planet**. It also took the Remnants' outdated [stargate](Stargates) technology. Only priority members of the species escaped, in a fleet of generation ships.
