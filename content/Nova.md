**Designation:** S37 · **Species:** Human ([Series 3 clone](Series-3-Clones)) · **Born:** lab-grown · **Role:** [later-generation mech](Later-Generation-Mechs) pilot · **Mech:** [BG3 M00 "Zero"](BRIDGE-Mechs#bg3-m00-p) · **Timeline:** Canon / [RHV](Timeline-RHV)

![030 cover art](images/030-cover.png)

A child soldier with an extremely high aptitude for piloting mechs, and one of the first successful pilots of the later-generation bio-tech mechs. Later considered one of the best pilots there has ever been.

## Appearance

* Pale skin and blue eyes
* A bit shorter than the twins, [Samantha](Samantha) and [Olivia](Olivia)
* **Label:** S37, on her right shoulder
* **Before the implants:** long silver hair reaching below her shoulders, usually tied behind her head or held back with a cap
* **After the implants:** hair kept shorter; neural-link ports in her upper back, neck and the back of her head
* **Scar:** along her right temple, from a mercenary's bullet the morning of her implant surgery

## Background

Nova was the third and last lab-grown clone, and the last "imperfect" clone, though she was still counted a success. At birth she had the **second-highest aptitude** of any clone, just behind [Adam](Adam).

Other imperfect clones with high aptitude paid for it with severe health problems. Nova is comparatively healthy:

* **Immune system:** incredibly strong, but she is **immune to most drugs**
* **Sun sensitivity**
* **No dreams:** she keeps some consciousness while she sleeps

The military values her highly and works to keep her separate from the other clones.

## Personality

Nova has trouble showing, and even feeling, most emotions, and her environment and military training reinforce it. She acts like an empty shell and works to her absolute limit on any command, believing her only purpose is whatever the military wants it to be. She is analytical, skilled and a good strategist.

Underneath, and despite denying it even to herself, she cares about the other clones, and even about the soldiers and scientists who mostly treat her as an object. She wishes she had been **born instead of made in a lab**, wanting the maternal bond the surrogate-born clones have with [Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov), never realising that Lana was trying to give her the same thing.

## Traits

* **Obedient:** she never talks back to her handlers
* **Emotionless on the surface:** a fixed, blank expression and a flat, monotone voice
* **Routine:** extremely precise; she lives by an unchanging daily schedule
* **Semi-conscious sleep:** she stays partly aware of her surroundings while asleep, as protection against ambush
* **Lethal:** deadly even without a mech, and armed
* **Shaking hand:** her right hand is always steady; her left hand shakes

## History

### The mercenaries

The morning of her implant surgery, Nova was stopped in an alley in the [Northern Facility City](Northern-Facility-City) by two mercenaries hired by [HAS](HAS). They had gone beyond their orders and set out to kill BRIDGE's most-used pilot. One held her at gunpoint while the other tried to assault her. They didn't know she was armed: she drew and shot them both. The gunman fired as he fell, and the bullet grazed her right temple. She collected her things and walked on to the hospital without changing her expression.

### Implant surgery

The doctors tried to delay the operation because of the head wound, but the military overruled them. The procedure was performed by [the Doctor](The-Doctor). Drugs meant to give patients pleasant dreams instead left Nova aware of the operation from outside her body, in a nightmarish vision of the room. She woke long afterwards with her hair grown out and the wound healed, and was ordered straight back to testing.

### BG3 M00

Nova was the pilot of **[BG3 M00 (P)](BRIDGE-Mechs#bg3-m00-p)**, the first direct-link mech built after G2M01, nicknamed **"Zero"**.

### BRIDGE-Fault

During [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault), Nova took the unarmed BG3 M00 against the repaired G2M01, which held [Daniel](Daniel) inside a modified G1M01 controlled by [Nina Lera](Nina-Lera). When Nina detonated her explosives, Nova prevented any deaths beyond the two people inside G2M01.

### The war and after ([RHV](Timeline-RHV))

Nova rises to become one of the deadliest pilots of the [Conglomerate War](Conglomerate-War). As the war ends she is **discarded**. She tries to help with rebuilding, but when [the Outbreak](The-Outbreak) begins her high aptitude gives her a resistance most pilots don't have. People are frightened of her, and her conflicting emotions let the virus take a toll: she feels out of her own skin whenever she isn't in her mech. Her absolute obedience means she still keeps to the mandated time outside the mech, which unknowingly slows the infection.

With the help of her first real friends among the other pilots, Nova learns to be herself and stop serving as a weapon. She fights back the infection, puts the infected mechs to rest, and uses her mech to help people rebuild instead of fighting.

Freedom comes with a cost. With her purpose gone, the guilt of everything she has done seeps in, and she falls into a deep depression, at times wanting to return to how things were despite how bad they were for her.

## Relationships

* **Siblings:** the other [Series 3 clones](Series-3-Clones). They all try to help her despite the distance between them.
* **[Samantha](Samantha):** protective of Nova
* **[Mila](Mila):** resents Nova for being the better pilot and getting the advanced tests, not realising Nova gets them because she's considered more expendable
* **[Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov):** tried to be a mother to her
* **The military:** isn't evil or corrupt, but never sees Nova as human, only as a weapon. Other pilots are treated as people and comrades.

## Home

A small two-room unit in the [Northern Facility City](Northern-Facility-City).

## Behind the scenes

* Nova's stories are **030** (the original version) and **BG3 Model 0, Pilot: S37-NOVA**, or "Zero". See [Stories](Stories).
* In the original 030 draft, Nova is described with extremely faded blonde hair, and her name is said to have been given after she was "found". The clone backstory replaces this.
* The story is meant to be anti-war. Infected mech use is written as an allegory for drug use, and Nova's time with the government leans heavily on body horror.
