**Designation:** # (S37: marked on her right shoulder) · **Species:** Human · **Role:** [later-generation mech](Later-Generation-Mechs) pilot · **Timeline:** Canon / [RHV](Timeline-RHV)

A child soldier with an extremely high affinity for piloting mechs, and one of the first successful pilots of the later-generation bio-tech mechs.

## Appearance

* Pale skin
* Deep blue eyes
* Label S37 marked on her right shoulder
* **Before getting neck implants**: long silver hair reaching below her shoulders.
* **After getting the neck implants**: metal neural-link ports in her lower neck and upper back

## Background

Nova was the third and last Lab-grown clone, and the last “imperfect” clone, though she was also considered a success. At birth, she had the second-highest aptitude. Nova is pretty healthy considering her aptitude, as all other “imperfect” clones with high aptitudes suffer severe drawbacks. Her immune system is incredibly strong, with the drawback of having immunity to most drugs and sun sensitivity. She doesn't dream while also keeping some consciousness in sleep. Nova has trouble showing, and even feeling, most emotions; the environment and military training have enforced this. She acts like an empty shell and works to her absolute limit following any command, believing her sole purpose is what the military wants it to be. She has some disconnection from the others because of her personality, though they all still try to help her; but because of how much the military values this version of her, they make efforts to separate her from the others. Although she seems to lack any emotions on the surface, even denying this herself, she cares for the other clones and even the military and scientists who mostly see and treat her as an object. She is still very analytical, but also skilled and good at strategizing. She wishes she was born instead of made in a lab, wanting the same maternal connection the surrogate-mother clones have to Lana, not realizing that Lana was trying to give that to her. Not only does Nova have a high aptitude, but she is also an incredibly skilled pilot, later considered one of the best. After BRIDGE-Fault, Nova finds herself in the Generation 3 prototype fighting the repaired G2M01, with a modified G1M01 wearing Daniel, who is being controlled by Nina Lera, also from inside the mech. This leads to the events of BG3 Model 0, Pilot: S37-NOVA (or just “Zero”). Before getting the neck implants to pilot, Nova had longer silver hair that went below her shoulders, and it was usually tied behind her head or held back with a cap, but afterward she kept it shorter. She has blue eyes, pale skin, and she is a bit shorter than the twins. Her Label is on her right shoulder, and she has linking implants on her upper back, neck, and the back of her head. She also has a scar along her right temple after being shot by a mercenary before the surgery.

## Home

A small two-room unit in the [Northern Facility City](Northern-Facility-City).

## Traits

* **Obedient:** she never talks back to her handlers
* **Emotionless on the surface:** a fixed, blank expression and a flat, monotone voice
* **Routine:** extremely precise; she lives by an unchanging daily schedule
* **Semi-conscious sleep:** a product of her training. She stays partly aware of her surroundings while asleep, as protection against ambush
* **Lethal:** deadly even without a mech
* **Shaking hand:** her right hand is always steady; her left hand shakes

## Treatment

The military isn't evil or corrupt, but it never sees Nova as human, only as a weapon. Other pilots are treated as people and comrades.
