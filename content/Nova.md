**Designation:** # (a test-subject number marked on her shoulder) · **Species:** Human · **Role:** [later-generation mech](Later-Generation-Mechs) pilot · **Timeline:** Canon / [RHV](Timeline-RHV)

A child soldier with an extremely high affinity for piloting mechs, and one of the first successful pilots of the later-generation bio-tech mechs.

## Appearance

- Early teens
- Pale skin
- Very faded blond hair, almost white, to her shoulders
- **Deep blue eyes**
- After her surgery, metal neural-link ports in her lower neck and upper back

## Background

"Nova" is not her birth name. It was given to her after she was "found", and it's the only name she has known. The military raised her to be the perfect soldier and the perfect test subject. She has only ever known war, government and the military. The [Conglomerate War](Conglomerate-War) began long before she was born.

She is far from the first subject of the program that made her.

## Home

A small two-room unit in the [Northern Facility City](Northern-Facility-City).

## Traits

- **Obedient:** she never talks back to her handlers
- **Emotionless on the surface:** a fixed, blank expression and a flat, monotone voice
- **Routine:** extremely precise; she lives by an unchanging daily schedule
- **Semi-conscious sleep:** a product of her training. She stays partly aware of her surroundings while asleep, as protection against ambush
- **Lethal:** deadly even without a mech
- **Shaking hand:** her right hand is always steady; her left hand shakes

## Treatment

The military isn't evil or corrupt, but it never sees Nova as human, only as a weapon. Other pilots are treated as people and comrades.
