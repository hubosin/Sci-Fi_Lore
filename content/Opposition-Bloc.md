**Type:** human political faction · **Formed:** January 1985 · **Timeline:** Canon

The part of [humanity](Humanity) that rejects the [Remnant-Human Alliance](Remnant-Human-Alliance).

## Members

- Countries that **left NATO** over the treaty
- Countries that **oppose** the alliance

## Beliefs

Opposition to the alliance takes several forms:

- **Deniers:** believe the Remnants don't exist, and that governments invented them as an excuse to overwork and underpay civilians for mass weapons manufacturing aimed at the rival bloc
- **Distrusters:** believe the Remnants are real but manipulating humanity to get its weapons technology
- **Conglomerate sympathisers:** a group that grows late in the war as humanity learns what the Conglomerate is fighting and where humanity stands in the grand scheme of things

## Key events

- **January 7–9, 1985:** humanity splits over the treaty
- **May 20, 1985:** mass protests against the alliance's war economy
