**Timeline:** [RHV](Timeline-RHV) · **Type:** artificial forest and human settlement zone

The Ring is a gigantic, almost perfectly circular forest created by the **last supercharged [RHHM](RHHM)** still running after the Conglomerate War. It's visible from space.

## Formation

During the war, RHHMs were supercharged to grow forest cover against Conglomerate attack. This one was left running at high power for so long that it grew an incredibly large, dense forest.

## The Ring itself

Strictly, "the Ring" is the **drop-off zone** at the forest's edge, where the RHHM's field weakens. That band happens to be a perfect human habitat, and **a large share of Earth's surviving humans live there**. Smaller, non-supercharged RHHMs elsewhere in the world usually have survivor settlements of their own.

## The inner forest

The forest inside the Ring can be crossed, but it's extremely dangerous: dense, repetitive, easy to get lost in, and enormous. There are no mutated creatures there, only ordinary dangerous animals, but **myths** of mutated monsters in the deep forest persist among the settlers. The RHHM itself sits at the centre.
