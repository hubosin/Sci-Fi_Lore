**Type:** slower-than-light propulsion · **Used by:** [Humanity](Humanity)

The standard propulsion system for human spacecraft.

## Travel times

With fusion-era technology:

- An Earth–Jupiter trip takes about **a month**.
- Accelerating at a constant **⅓ G** with the planets closely aligned, it takes about **11 days**.
