**Type:** measurement standard

A standard for measuring time between species. It is based on the **half-life of a specific element** and scaled up and down like the metric system.

## Reference element

**Californium-252**, with a half-life of **2.645 years**.

## Significance

Californium-252 is hard to obtain, so being able to use the standard is one way advanced species gauge how advanced another species is.
