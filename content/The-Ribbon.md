**Callsign:** The Ribbon · **Species:** Human · **Service:** U.S. Air Force (retired) · **Role:** fighter ace · **Timeline:** [RHV](Timeline-RHV)

A retired **USAF ace** living in **Western Alaska** when the Conglomerate lands.

## Service in the war

- Returned to service after Conglomerate scouts landed north of Denali, first on the ground and trained in **guerrilla warfare**, then back in the air
- Flew outdated **VTOL aircraft** from covert forest bases
- Trained new pilots
- Became personally targeted by the Conglomerate once it found his records, because Earth's air forces had beaten its forces too often to leave him alive
