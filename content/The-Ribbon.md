**Callsign:** The Ribbon · **Species:** Human · **Service:** U.S. Air Force (retired), later Soviet patrols · **Role:** fighter ace · **Timeline:** [RHV](Timeline-RHV)

A retired **USAF ace** living in **Western Alaska** when the Conglomerate lands. He is the protagonist of *A Ribbon Sheltered*.

## Service in the war

### Back on the ground

When Conglomerate scouts landed north of Denali National Park, the Ribbon volunteered to fight again, this time on the ground, and was trained in **guerrilla warfare**. After a Conglomerate attack destroyed an air force base, the Conglomerate found his records. Earth's air forces had beaten it too many times for it to risk leaving him alive.

### Capture and escape

During a botched attack on a Conglomerate outpost he was captured and imprisoned. Recognised as the Ribbon, he was set to be executed, but barely escaped.

### Back in the air

Now personally hunted, he returned to air combat, flying outdated **VTOL aircraft** from makeshift covert forest bases. He flew increasingly risky missions and took the newest pilots along for training, and he always brought everyone back alive, which seemed to anger his commander.

### Betrayal

After two of his trainees were shot down, he discovered that his commander, and most of his fellow pilots, were plotting against him because he drew Conglomerate attention to their squadrons. After a failed attempt on his life he stole an aircraft and flew for the **Bering Strait**. On the way he was intercepted by two aircraft from his base: his two surviving students. The three evaded both the Conglomerate and their former allies.

### The Soviet base

A **Soviet air patrol** found them and led them to a hidden airbase, where the commander agreed to shelter them. They joined the Soviet patrols and built a reputation in both the Soviet and American militaries in the region. They also started new lives in the small town by the base, forming friendships and relationships with the locals.

### The last battle

Near the end of the Conglomerate presence on Earth, a large Conglomerate force landed and began mining, torching the forests and spreading toward the town. A combined **US-Soviet** attack was launched, and the three pilots were scrambled. After a long fight the Conglomerate infrastructure was destroyed, obliterating the landscape around it. Wildfire smoke forced the pilots to land elsewhere, and they only returned days later, to debris-strewn runways.

## Ending

The Ribbon walks through the charred ruins of the town. He is alive and the Conglomerate has been driven out, but his community and everyone in it are gone.
