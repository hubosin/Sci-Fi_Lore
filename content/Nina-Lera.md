**Role:** surrogate mother of [Series 3](Series-3-Clones) · **Affiliation:** Russian government · **Species:** Human · **Died:** [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault) · **Timeline:** Canon

The second Series 3 surrogate, and the person behind [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault).

## Role

Nina joined the program through the **Russian government** to test whether clones turned out differently with a different surrogate. She was the birth mother of [Mila](Mila), the first "perfect" clone.

## Personality

Incredibly self-centred, but devoted to her country. Her biggest goal was to be **important**. She saw all the clones as something less than human. She had some connection to Mila, but mainly because Mila was such a striking success.

## Motive

Producing the first perfect clone straight after joining left Nina riding high. She believed she had made [Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov) redundant and was frustrated when Lana was kept on. When Lana gave birth to [Daniel](Daniel), another perfect clone, it proved the process worked with any surrogate. Nina was furious to find that **she** was now the insignificant one, and came to hate Daniel and Lana.

## BRIDGE-Fault

Nina tricked Daniel into a modified G1M01, took him into the repaired G2M01 packed with explosives, and controlled his movements while he was linked to the mech. When Daniel shut G2M01 down anyway, she detonated the explosives, killing them both. Her attack also included an attempt on Lana's life. See [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault).
