## Bomb Defusal Crew

**Timeline:** [I-Line](Timeline-I-Line) · **Set:** up to two years after first contact

Follows one member, or all, of the multinational crew sent in a human-built, Remnant-designed ship to stop the bomb sent after the Remnant fleet. The climax is a crew member sacrificing themself to **detonate the bomb early**. The survivors escort the Remnant fleet to Mars, where the monarch and Earth's leaders form the alliance. A late twist reveals early detonation was always the plan: classified weapons advances made during the science race meant disarming it in time was impossible.

**Feel:** alien and awe-inspiring. The ship seems like ordinary advanced human tech until the crew discovers what it can really do. The first sight of the Remnant fleet should be megalophobic: a tiny human ship, a looming Remnant vessel, a nearby moon, and Jupiter filling the frame.

## 030

**Timeline:** [RHV](Timeline-RHV) · **Status:** original version of Nova's story; short story and cover art by Jack Ireland

Opens with Nova's precise morning routine in the [Northern Facility City](Northern-Facility-City), an encounter with two armed men in an alley, her implant surgery and its nightmare vision, and her first boot-up in a bio-tech mech. The full arc follows her rise as a weapon, her abandonment after the war, the [Outbreak](The-Outbreak), and her slow discovery of who she is.

**Planned moments:**

* The boot-up of a different pilot, treated as a human and a comrade, in contrast to Nova
* Nova handing her human troop government ID devices, because she will eliminate every armed life sign she scans, said in a flat monotone
* Growing awareness of emotion, and guilt over the people she has killed
* Infected mech use as an allegory for drug use

**Feel:** anti-war. No glory in battle, only the completion of the task. Victories are short, the enemy always has a family, and innocents always die. Heavy body horror during Nova's time with the government.

## A Ribbon Sheltered

**Timeline:** [RHV](Timeline-RHV), in a version where the Conglomerate can't get its strongest machines to Earth

A retired USAF ace in Western Alaska is drawn back into the war, captured, betrayed by his own commander, and sheltered by a Soviet airbase, until a final battle destroys the community he built. See [The Ribbon](The-Ribbon) and [Alaska and the Russian Far East](Alaska-and-the-Russian-Far-East).

## Embers in a Dead Flame

**Timeline:** [RHV](Timeline-RHV) · **Format:** book, or possibly a game

One of the last first-generation pilots climbs back into their mech, possibly with a small crew including a **Remnant prince**, to fight the infected later-generation mechs after [the Outbreak](The-Outbreak).

**Protagonist options:**

* One of the war's best pilots, who kept their identity hidden and lived quietly until called back
* The child of a pilot who died in the war and was a hero to the Remnants. The parent built a mech with the help of the Remnant royal family, and the child and the prince use it to save humanity.
* A returning crew of pilots, which could include either of the above. As a game, character choice or multiplayer would work.

**Plot:** fights grow harder as the infection gains intelligence and strategy. Mercenaries and pirates fly stolen or patched-together mechs. A turning point is finding a later-generation mech that is **resisting** the infection, learning why, and replicating it.

**Ending:** bittersweet. The enemy is defeated but humanity is in tatters, though with hope for the future.

**Feel:** gritty and realistic. The machines are down to earth, and the enemies are monstrous and alien.

## Tholanthanar

**Timeline:** [RHV](Timeline-RHV), at the same time as *Embers in a Dead Flame* · **Inspired by:** Arum's backstory

A later-generation pilot is infected on missions against infected mechs. They suffer sickness and waking nightmares and only feel safe inside the mech, so they leave the military and slowly recover, starting a relationship. The pilot's mech, fully infected and rejecting every new pilot, escapes testing and tears apart their house looking for them.

Following it, the pilot finds what seems to be its wreck (defeated by the protagonist of *Embers in a Dead Flame*, which revealed it was running with no pilot at all). The mech pulls them in and integrates them. Much later the pilot regains control just before killing their partner. The resistance they built up while living normally lets them slowly fight back, regaining fragments of consciousness, especially in places and near people from their life, without truly remembering any of it.

**Feel:** horror, especially body horror, and fear of the unknown, ending in sadness: the pilot is back, but trapped in the mech and unable to remember the people they love.

## Acceptance

**Timeline:** [EACO](Timeline-EACO) · **Format:** choose-your-own-adventure book

A late-war pilot who believes in the Conglomerate is put into [stasis](Stasis), feeling a tremor and a sense that something has gone wrong as everything goes dark. They wake early on a mostly empty transport ship in a wormhole, then deploy with their unit to a "resisting planet".

**The twist:** the planet is **Earth**, drained and strip-mined beyond recognition, and the enemy is [the Survivors](The-Survivors). In the ending the story was built around, the pilot, unaware, kills every last target and is abandoned by the Conglomerate. They climb out of the wrecked mech into a recognisable city, likely New York, see the Statue of Liberty, and realise they have killed the last of their own people.

**Design:** hard to die, with common sense leading to well-earned endings; some death routes reveal information; options that skip information lead fastest to the bad ending. The reader might be the pilot, a handler or copilot, or a godlike outside influence.

## Discordant Purpose

**Timeline:** N/A

A handful of characters caught in a [Resonant Virulon](Resonant-Virulon) takeover of a forgotten system try to reach a ship that can get them to an Ordinal Virulon system. Set in the [Cygnus System](Cygnus-System), whose capital **Ciroth** researches bio-augments.
