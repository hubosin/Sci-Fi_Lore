**Type:** classified mech and neural-link program · **Also called:** BRIDGE, the Link Program · **Run by:** Soviet scientists, later the U.S. and Soviet governments jointly · **Timeline:** Canon (Conglomerate War onward)

The BRIDGE Project is the secret program that turned captured Conglomerate neural-link technology into humanity's single-pilot [later-generation mechs](Later-Generation-Mechs).

## Overview

It began as **the Link Program**, a small project started by Soviet scientists. It became **BRIDGE** when the American and Soviet governments took it over together, **without the Remnants' knowledge**. After the takeover the two names stuck to two halves of the work:

* **BRIDGE** focused on building deadlier mechs, using neural connection for more speed and accuracy and a lower, less-trained pilot requirement.
* **The Link Program** focused on finding pilots. See [Pilot Series](Pilot-Series).

Publicly, BRIDGE was a philanthropy project building prosthetics and mechs for restoration work. In reality it was developing advanced weaponry.

## History

### The Link Program

After the alliance captured Conglomerate technology, a small team of Soviet scientists started a project to reverse-engineer the **neural connection** that controls most Conglomerate mechs and ships, and to turn it into medical technology. Most of its budget came out of the team members' own pockets. Although it was run as a passion project, most of the team still reported directly to the government.

The first things it built were **prosthetics**, now classed as generation 0. With added funding the team moved on to larger prototypes:

1. **First wave ([Generation 1](BRIDGE-Mechs#generation-1)):** mechanical recreations of properties observed in Conglomerate tech. Clunky and slow by later standards.
2. **Second wave ([Generation 2](BRIDGE-Mechs#generation-2)):** real Conglomerate tech with integrated human technology. Only one was ever built, [LPG2 M01](BRIDGE-Mechs#lpg2-m01).

### The G2M01 incident

[The Engineer](The-Engineer) who designed the direct-link system volunteered to pilot G2M01. On start-up the mech suffered a power fluctuation that overrode the limits protecting the pilot. It rampaged through the lab, faster and more destructive than anything built before, and the Engineer was found dead in the cockpit after an exterior shutdown.

### Government takeover

The incident proved a single linked pilot could control enormous power, even in a pain-driven rampage. The American and Soviet governments, who had already been running a similar project behind the scenes, took the program over and renamed it **BRIDGE**. Link Program staff were forced to stay on.

### Expansion

Under BRIDGE the program tested pilots on a massive scale, first with paid, expendable volunteers ([Series 2](Pilot-Series#series-2)) and then with genetically engineered clones ([Series 3](Series-3-Clones)). Generation 4 mechs entered service before Generation 3, which was kept for testing. As the program gained traction it lost much of its secrecy, and by [Series 4](Pilot-Series#series-4) being a pilot was something people wanted.

### Opposition

The thousands of Series 2 disappearances drew the attention of [HAS](HAS), which sent people to investigate and shut down BRIDGE's weapons work. Two mercenaries hired by HAS later tried to kill [Nova](Nova).

### BRIDGE-Fault

The program's worst internal disaster, in which the repaired G2M01 was turned against BRIDGE from within. See [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault).

## Designation system

BRIDGE mechs are named by program, generation and model: **LPG# M#** for Link Program machines and **BG# M#** for BRIDGE machines. A short form drops the program: **G#M#**. A **(P)** marks a prototype. See [BRIDGE Mechs](BRIDGE-Mechs).

## Aptitude

**Aptitude** is how well a person can link with a mech. [Series 2](Pilot-Series#series-2) showed it depends heavily on **brain elasticity**, so younger pilots score higher. Aptitude changes as the brain develops, but after a first link connection it declines much more slowly, and with training it can stabilise or even improve. See [Pilot Series](Pilot-Series#aptitude).
