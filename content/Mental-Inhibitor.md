**Type:** control implant · **Used by:** [the Virulon](The-Virulon)

The device the Virulon use to control their slaves.

## Function

A mental inhibitor is implanted into the **spine** (or equivalent) of a slave. It severs the connection between body and brain and takes over **all movement**, putting the slave under the Virulon's absolute control.

## Permanence

Over time the implant forces the body to change and **absorbs it into the host's body**. After that it can't be removed without killing the slave.
