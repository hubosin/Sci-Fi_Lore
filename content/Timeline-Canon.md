Dates in **bold** match real-world history. Everything else is where this universe diverges.

## Divergence (1945–1983)

- **September 2, 1945:** World War II ends and the Cold War begins
- **April 12, 1961:** The space race begins with Yuri Gagarin's flight on Vostok 1
- **July 20, 1969:** Apollo 11 lands on the Moon, "winning" the space race
- August 3, 1971: First Soviet Moon landing
- June 19, 1973: First net-positive fusion reaction
- **July 17, 1975:** Apollo-Soyuz docking, the official end of the space race. The rivalry carries on as the [science race](Science-Race)
- November 8, 1975: First crewed flyby of Mars launches
- July 4, 1976: The first space warship, the [USS *Independence*](USS-Independence), is unveiled
- 1981: The Soviet [MAKS](MAKS) air-launched spaceplane is proposed
- February 2, 1982: The joint NASA/Roscosmos [*Intrepid*](Intrepid-Mission) mission lands the first humans on Mars
- November 22, 1982: Mikhail Gorbachev is elected Soviet General Secretary

## First contact (1984)

See [First Contact](First-Contact) for the full account.

- **September 8, 1984:** The Europa probe [*Aurora 2*](Aurora-2) sights the Remnant fleet
- **September 13, 1984:** The Remnant fleet's trajectory is mapped and Earth warships are deployed
- **September 24, 1984:** A Remnant scout convoy reaches Earth undetected and is destroyed by Earth's militaries
- **September 27, 1984:** Radio contact is established with the main Remnant force
- **October 4, 1984:** The Remnant learns the English language and communication is established
- **October 5, 1984:** The U.S. and the USSR retask their deployed warships to escort the Remnant fleet
- **October 17, 1984:** The American/Soviet fleet meets the Remnant fleet in the inner asteroid belt
- **October 29, 1984:** The Remnant fleet, with its escorts, reaches a holding pattern over Mars
- **November 11, 1984:** A Remnant diplomatic squadron reaches Earth

## Interwar period (1984–the war)

- November 20, 1984: Remnant leadership (the [monarch](Remnant-Monarch) and elected officials) addresses the U.N. about the need to cooperate against an overpowering threat
- January 6, 1985: The [Remnant-Human Alliance](Remnant-Human-Alliance) becomes official. Inaugural signees include most NATO members, the USSR, and Japan
- January 7–9, 1985: Humanity splits into two factions: those who support the treaty and those who think the Remnant is tricking them
- January 12, 1985: Ground is broken on massive manufacturing sites based on Remnant technology in North America and the USSR. The [second industrial age](Second-Industrial-Age) begins
- May 20, 1985: Mass protests break out as citizens suspect their governments of inventing the Remnant to restart the war economy
- The [RHHM](RHHM) is developed as a joint philanthropy project

## The Conglomerate War

See [The Conglomerate War](Conglomerate-War).

- A team observing through a [solar gravitational lens](Solar-Gravitational-Lens) confirms the Conglomerate fleet is heading for Earth
- The war begins and lasts well over a decade
- The second Conglomerate attack destroys most of Earth's forests. The alliance begins supercharging RHHMs to grow cover
- After the alliance captures Conglomerate technology, Soviet scientists start the Link Program to reverse-engineer its neural link. See [BRIDGE Project](BRIDGE-Project)
- The [G2M01](BRIDGE-Mechs#lpg2-m01) incident kills the Engineer. The U.S. and Soviet governments take over the program as **BRIDGE**
- [Series 2](Pilot-Series#series-2) testing: tens of thousands of paid subjects, more than half of whom die. The disappearances draw [HAS](HAS) to BRIDGE
- The [Series 3 clones](Series-3-Clones) are born (S31–S39), among them [Nova](Nova)
- [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault): Nina Lera hijacks the repaired G2M01; Daniel (S39) dies and Series 3 ends
- [Series 4](Pilot-Series#series-4): aptitude-tested volunteers; becoming a pilot is now popular
- Late in the war, bio-tech from the Conglomerate is integrated into the first [later-generation mechs](Later-Generation-Mechs)
- The Conglomerate proposes a compromise. **The timeline branches:** [RHV](Timeline-RHV) · [EACO](Timeline-EACO) · [Red Line](Timeline-Red-Line)
