**Designation:** S39 · **Species:** Human ([Series 3 clone](Series-3-Clones)) · **Born:** surrogate ([Lana Morozov](Lana-Morozov)) · **Died:** [BRIDGE-Fault](BRIDGE-Fault) · **Timeline:** Canon

The last Series 3 clone. His birth to Lana proved the cloning process had been **perfected** and could work with any surrogate, so clones could go into larger production if funding was approved.

## Appearance

* Mid-length faded blonde hair, deep blue eyes
* Small
* **Label:** S39, on his right shoulder
* Advanced linking implants on his upper back, neck and the back of his head

## Personality

Like [Mila](Mila), he was spoiled when younger, and like her he grew more humble with age. He became good at lifting other people up. He could be difficult and oblivious, but he was a good kid.

## Relationships

* **[Mila](Mila):** close friend; he was for her what [Rose](Rose) was for Adam
* **[Nina Lera](Nina-Lera):** hated him for ending her fantasy of being irreplaceable

## Death

Daniel was told he would be testing an early prototype and was tricked into a **modified [LPG1 M01](BRIDGE-Mechs#lpg1-m01)**. Nina drove him into the repaired [G2M01](BRIDGE-Mechs#lpg2-m01) and controlled his body through G1M01's helmet while he was linked to G2M01.

The link at such a young age caused severe mental strain and **brain damage**. Even so, Daniel managed to **shut G2M01 down**. Nina then detonated the explosives packed into the mech, killing them both.

Series 3 ended with his death.
