**Type:** FTL drive · **Used by:** [Humanity](Humanity)

Humanity's faster-than-light drive. It warps space around the ship instead of moving the ship through space.

## Advantages

- **No fixed endpoints:** ships can travel to and from anywhere, unlike stargates.
- **Easy to mass-produce.**
- **Bypasses time dilation,** so Alcubierre ships reach destinations faster than ships using other FTL methods.

## Why humanity uses it

There are no natural wormholes near Sol, so humans couldn't copy the stargate approach.
