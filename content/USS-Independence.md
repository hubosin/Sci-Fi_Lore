**Type:** space warship · **Operator:** United States · **Unveiled:** July 4, 1976 · **Timeline:** Canon

Humanity's **first space warship**, unveiled on the U.S. bicentennial, the year after the Apollo-Soyuz docking ended the space race and the [science race](Science-Race) took its place.

## Significance

It showed that the science race had never been purely peaceful. Military space technology kept advancing alongside the scientific rivalry, and by 1984 Earth had warships to send against the Remnant fleet. See [First Contact](First-Contact).
